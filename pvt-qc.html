How points are flagged

  • Off-trend (robust z). Each property is fitted against pressure with a robust polynomial (iteratively re-weighted, Tukey bisquare), so one bad point cannot drag the line toward itself. Residuals are scaled by the median absolute deviation, with a floor at half the last reported digit so rounding is never flagged. Points with |z| above the threshold are flagged. Excluded points are left out of the fit. Change the trend order per chart if the curve shape needs it.
  • CCE. V/Vsat must be ≤ 1 above and ≥ 1 below Psat and must grow as pressure drops. For oils the Y-function should be a straight line; points close to Psat are marked as information only, because Y is very sensitive there. For condensates, P·Vrel (proportional to two-phase Z) and liquid dropout are trend-checked.
  • CVD. Liquid dropout, gas Z and moles produced are trend-checked. Two-phase Z is compared with p/Z₂ₚₕ = (Pd/Zd)(1 − nₚ). With gas compositions, liquid compositions are back-calculated stage by stage; a negative mole fraction, a curved Hoffman line, or a wrong-sign slope flags that stage's gas analysis.
  • DL. Rs and Bo must fall as pressure drops below Pb; Bo vs Rs should be nearly straight; oil density is compared with a mass balance built from residual-oil density, ΔRs and liberated gas gravity.
  • Use judgement. A flag is a reason to look, not a verdict. Exclude a point when there is a physical reason it is wrong, not only because the EOS can't match it.
